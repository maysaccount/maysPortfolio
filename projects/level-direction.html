Pastel mountain — 2.5D level

A winter platformer level in a pastel palette, blocked out in Unity so the objects read clearly against the sky.

UnityConcept art

Art direction

2.5D so 3D models can live in a 2D platformer. Yellow planks, white rock and grey buildings stand out against the pink-to-blue sky, so the player always sees the route. A flag marks the finish the moment you enter the level.

Leading the player

The higher you climb the further you've progressed, like a real hike. Planks line up with the cable car so you know it's the next step, and doors are always open so you know you can go through them.
