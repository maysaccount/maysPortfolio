2D Asteroids

Shoot the floating space rocks with your rocket. Crash into one and you lose a life; lose three and it's over.

UnityC#

Player controls

Speed and turn speed are applied through the Rigidbody on the specific axis. Pressing space instantiates a bullet from the ship's current position and rotation. If a bullet touches an object with the enemy prefab it destroys it and adds 10 to the score.

Screen wrap and clean-up

If the player goes off the top of the screen they reappear at the bottom. If an asteroid or bullet goes off screen it gets destroyed so nothing useless keeps running.

Asteroids

Asteroids spawn at a random point below the map and float up to the top. Touching the player takes a life and respawns the ship in the middle so the player notices. Touching a bullet destroys the bullet so it's removed from the scene.

Score and lives

Score and lives are stored as text objects so they update when the code does its thing. At 0 lives the game shows a “You Lose” message until the scene reloads. It also disables the controls script and removes gravity, so the ship falls into the void for a fun death animation.
