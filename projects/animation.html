Walk cycle and flip

A rigged character with a confident walk cycle and a martial-arts flip, blocked out as key poses then cleaned up.

BlenderUnity

The walk

A steady walk in a cartoony style, which gives freedom to show emotion through the action. Blocking sets a key pose for each step so you can see where the character ends up, then the in-between frames get filled in. The step goes upwards and the arms swing to show energy; the head stays up to show the character isn't worried.

The flip

Inspired by martial-arts and action media. The blocking starts from a rest pose, steps the left leg forward for momentum, then changes the arm and hip positions between key frames 2 and 3 to set up the flip. I learned the IK/FK switch here: the feet had to go to FK so they follow the hips without the animation breaking.
