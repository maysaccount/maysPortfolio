Puzzle Labyrinth

An escape-room puzzle game with story elements, set in liminal spaces. Pitched to the class and revised after feedback.

Pitch deckGame design document

The hook

The escape-room company invited you to take part in their experience. The further in you get, the more obvious it is they're not going to let you out any time soon. Rooms are 2D with clickable arrows to look left and right; objects are realistic 3D renders used as sprites.

Mechanics

Padlocks that need the right key dragged onto them. Jigsaw shards that assemble into a clue (a clock reading 7pm). Encrypted notes and a cipher. Invisible ink you can only read with a black-light tool. Three boxes and only one holds the code, so you have to put the clues together.

What I'd change

Feedback said the concept was good and simple, and that having no consistent art style made it stand out. But “target audience is everyone” isn't an answer, and the pitch needed more pictures and more mechanics. Someone asked if it was inspired by Blue Prince, so I looked into it: not similar enough to be a problem, but worth checking before building.
