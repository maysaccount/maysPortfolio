Dungeon escape — 3D prototype

Trapped underground, guarded by knights. A third-person controller with sprint and crouch, a health system, and melee attacks that find the nearest enemy.

UnityC#Blender

The idea

You're trapped in a dungeon and have to escape. WASD to walk, Ctrl to crawl under short gaps, which opens up routes you couldn't reach before. Doors stay locked until every enemy in the area is beaten and you're holding the key. Some areas are only reachable across bridges, and one misstep into the lava drops your health to zero.

Third-person movement

A CharacterController with gravity, jump height, walk and sprint speed, and a crouch that lerps the controller height and the camera down. isGrounded is a Physics.Raycast straight down, so the jump only works when you're actually standing on something.

Health and damage

Max health is 100. TakeDamage subtracts the amount, updates the health bar, and calls Die() when it reaches 0. When the player dies they respawn at the last checkpoint with health reset.

Attacking the nearest enemy

Left click finds every EnemyHealth in the scene, measures the distance to each, and damages the first one inside the equipped weapon's range. A cooldown based on the weapon's speed stops you spamming it.

Typed up: AttackNearbyEnemies() — who's in range?
