Controls.cs · 2D Asteroids
  1. 1Read the input. GetAxis gives a number between -1 and 1 for the arrow keys, so one line handles left and right.
  2. 2Move with physics, not teleport. MovePosition on the Rigidbody2D keeps collisions working. The transform.Translate lines are still there, commented out, from the first attempt.
  3. 3Spawn a bullet. Instantiate copies the ammo prefab at the ship's position and rotation, so it flies the way the ship is facing.
